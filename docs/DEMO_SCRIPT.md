# Three-minute submission demo

**Format:** Bangla narration with English subtitles · 16:9 screen capture · 3:00 total

**Story:** one sample farmer faces one seasonal choice; compare options, show the evidence and limits, then end with a practical next step.
**On screen:** use the live app and the demo account. Rahim and his field are sample data; say so before the first recommendation is shown.

## Recording checklist

- Record the live app at 1920×1080 or 1280×720, 30 fps, with browser chrome hidden. Keep the cursor slow and move only when the narration changes topic.
- Use the English subtitle column as burned-in captions. Keep each caption to two lines, high contrast, and inside the central 80% of the frame; proofread the Bangla names and crop names before export.
- Keep the app's provenance badges visible whenever a result is shown. Do not use stock footage to imply that sample farmers or sample fields are real.
- Record the narration in a quiet room, then check the exported video on a phone speaker and a laptop. Leave a short pause between scenes; do not speed-read to fit the timestamps.
- If any screen has changed since this script was checked, update the shot and re-record. Store the MP4 locally and on a USB drive; add an unlisted link only in the submission form.

## Before recording

- Open the live site once on the demo device so committed data and images are cached; then check the flow with the network disconnected.
- Reset the demo account and confirm the default farmer is Rahim at Godagari. Confirm that all three strategies, provenance labels and the eight-model test load before recording.
- Keep the recording focused on the challenge: NASA observations, local soil and crop information, farmer priorities, rotation trade-offs and the need for local review.
