# Demo script (3 minutes) and fallback

One farmer, one field, one decision first; the evidence behind it; what is real; then the ten-year view and the
rest. In the app, **3-minute demo story** on the home page follows the same order (`src/components/tour/DemoTour.jsx`).

| Time | Screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Season plan, step 5: **What you grow now and the alternatives** (Rahim, Godagari) | "Rahim farms one field on the Barind. What should he sow this Rabi? Here is what he grows now beside two alternatives, on the same 25 seasons of NASA weather, the same prices, the same field. The coloured bar shows why each ranks where it does." |
| 0:20–0:40 | **The first step, and what to check** | "Which crop, which week, and what to check in the field first: a soil test, because the soil numbers are a model estimate; seed; the irrigation turn. One tap sends the comparison to his agriculture officer." |
| 0:40–1:05 | **How this recommendation was made** | "What went into the calculation and what is only context. The crop rules are starting values that local agronomists have not reviewed yet, and we say so. And how firm it is: the same answer under other weights and in hotter or drier weather. When the lead is small, the app calls it a close call." |
| 1:05–1:25 | Science page, **Data coverage and gaps**, then **Where the sources disagree** | "Every source labelled: satellite, station, model, published record, rule or sample, with its years and scale. GRACE is a regional average, never a measurement of Rahim's field. Here is where rain from two NASA sources disagrees, and by how much." |
| 1:25–2:05 | Field Future: **soil time machine**, **side by side**, **does any rotation really help the soil?** | "Ten years of every feasible rotation on this field: soil carbon, nitrogen, water, cover and the fodder trade-off. Where no rotation clearly helps the soil, it says so." |
| 2:05–2:30 | Field Future: **eight-model test** | "The best rotations re-run in each of eight NASA climate models and five real yield years: what holds in any future, and what rests on an assumption." |
| 2:30–3:00 | Voice: "বিকল্পগুলোর তুলনা"; then officer view | "The same comparison by voice or SMS, with how sure it is and how to reach a person. The officer sees the farmer's question and the whole upazila." |

Say once, early: **the farmers and their plans are sample people; the weather, satellites, prices and yields are
real; the soil is a model estimate.**

## Before the demo

- Open the live site once on the demo device so the NASA weather and photos are cached; then test in airplane mode.
- Reset the demo data (Profile → reset) so Rahim starts at the season plan with no priorities picked.
- Keep the fallback recording on the device itself (not only in the cloud).

## Fallback recording

If the network or the device fails, play the recording instead of the live app. Record it from the live site with
the demo story running, at 1280×800, with the voice-over above, about 3 minutes. Keep a copy as MP4 on the laptop,
on a USB stick, and as an unlisted video link in the submission. Re-record after any change to the screens above.
